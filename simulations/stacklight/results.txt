All four AO3400A drains (pin 3) connect to +12V.
Gates are pin 1; sources are pin 2. No gate pulldowns were found.
The +12V net contains only the four drains in the exported netlist;
ensure the real assembly has a defined 12V feed. J12 has four channels,
so the lamp common and supply connection must be accounted for separately.
KiCad warned about annotation errors during netlist export.

Assumptions:
12V supply; 0/3.3V GPIO; 120 ohm resistive load (100mA at 12V).
Illustrative level-1 N-MOS model, VTO=1.05V, KP=40, RD=RS=15mOhm,
plus explicit body diode. This is NOT a manufacturer AO3400A model.
Its approximate low-voltage on resistance is consistent with the scale
of the datasheet but is not a guaranteed device prediction.
Low-side proposal includes a 100k gate-source pulldown and existing 1k
series gate resistance. Temperature is ngspice default 27C.

Literal source=+12V, drain=load-to-ground case:
Output is 11.287434V with gate low and 11.287435V with gate high.
The body diode powers the load even when the channel is off.
VGS is -12V at gate low, at the AO3400A absolute maximum magnitude,
with no tolerance for supply variation or transients.

Existing source-follower load current at gate high: about 18.5mA.
Existing MOSFET dissipation in that illustrative operating point: 181mW.
Proposed low-side load current at gate high: about 100mA.
Proposed low-side MOSFET dissipation: about 0.413mW.
The transient file exercises repeated GPIO low/high states over 6ms.
No capacitances or inductances are included: edge speed, gate charge,
inrush, ringing, flyback and switching losses are NOT validated.

Interpretation:
For a common-positive lamp: connect lamp common to +12V, AO3400A
source pin 2 to GND, drain pin 3 to the channel input, and gate pin 1
through the series resistor to GPIO. Share MCU and 12V supply ground.
Add gate-to-source pulldowns so reset/high-impedance GPIO leaves it off.
GPIO high turns the channel on; GPIO low turns it off. The drain is
near ground when on and near +12V when off through the assumed load.
Do not hard-short a driven GPIO to an opposing supply rail.

For a common-ground lamp requiring +12V inputs: use a high-side driver
or a suitable P-MOSFET plus level-shifting circuit. A 3.3V GPIO cannot
directly drive this AO3400A to deliver 12V from its source.
For an inductive sounder/load, evaluate a flyback/clamp circuit using
its actual specifications before considering the design complete.
